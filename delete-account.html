ST ENGINE – Account Deletion

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Users of ST ENGINE can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by following the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to support@stcbroker.id.
  2. Use the subject: ST ENGINE Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your registered account ID and the email address associated with your account, if applicable.
  4. Our support team may contact you to verify your request.

Data Deletion

After your request is verified, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account, unless certain information must be retained for legal, security, or legitimate operational purposes.

Processing Time

We aim to process verified account deletion requests within 30 days.
